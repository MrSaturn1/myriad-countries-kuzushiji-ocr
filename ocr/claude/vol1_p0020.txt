## notes
Summary: The left-page illustration is captioned "Russian peasants diligently pursuing the way of farming" (魯西亞の百姓耕作の道を励む), showing a man leading a plough-horse and a seated man under palm trees on the right. The running text (continued from the previous spread, marked つゞき, and continuing to the next, marked つゞく) seems to deal with tree-planting and grafting (うゑ木, ゑだのきりやう "how to cut branches", つぎ) and farming matters such as beans/crops and paying the annual rice tax (ねんぐ … おさむる). This summary is tentative.
Reading order: right top block (つゞき…) → left top block (ends ▲) → right bottom block (▲み…) → left bottom block (ends つゞく).
Self-estimate: about 35–45% character accuracy on the main text and about 90% on the caption. The highly cursive, run-together hentaigana in all the main-text blocks was hard, and in many columns I could only transcribe by letter shape without making sense of the words. The repeated stacked glyph is read as ゑ (it fits ゑだ 枝 and うゑ木 植木) but may be another kana. The column in the gutter between the pages is mostly hidden. The 柱/丁付 at both fore-edges are cut off by the binding.
