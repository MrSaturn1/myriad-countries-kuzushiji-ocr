## notes
Summary: Opening spread of a new section (title cartouches 魯文作 / 芳虎画). The right page continues a narrative about the Americans (めりけん) and the King (国王) — something is seized (うばひとつて) and the thread is marked as continuing in volume 3 (三の巻へ) — over a picture of a crowded rowing boat in a stormy sea; the left page, continued "from volume 2" (二の巻より), mentions the Queen (女王), America, the exile (配流) of men and women (男女), about 300 people (三百), and ships on what appears to be the Thames (てむせ川) near London, illustrated with a large warship and sailing ships, ending つゞく.
Self-estimate: roughly 40–50% character accuracy overall. Kanji (魯文作, 芳虎画, 女王, 国王, 配流, 男女, 萬死, 三百, 川, 一ツ) and the box labels (つゞき, 二の巻より, 三の巻へ, つゞく) are reliable, and so are several kana runs (ひまをうかゞひ, めりけんへ, ひとまのうちよ, うばひとつて, ところのうち, をよせんと, てむせ川を). The dense small cursive kana of the right page's upper block and lower-right block is the hardest part (~35%), along with hentaigana whose readings I could not settle: a 且/耳-shaped glyph (read as に), a ホ/尓-shaped glyph (read as に or ふ), 宇 (read as う), and 書/寿 (read as す). The page-margin headers are cut off at the fold and largely illegible; the dakuten and many single glyphs are guesses.
