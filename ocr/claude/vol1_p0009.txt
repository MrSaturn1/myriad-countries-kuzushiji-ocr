## notes
Summary: The right page opens with a boxed header giving figures for a European capital city: about 713 streets, 30,000 houses and a population of 600,000. The main text then seems to describe the city's buildings, how the country is governed, royal succession by bloodline with male and female heirs mentioned, and the French people ("ふらんすの人"). The lower blocks describe many schools (がくもんしよ, "academies") and teaching. The left page continues in the same vein and has a faint caption reading "○ふらんす国 … の図", so the picture is probably a view of a French city (Paris?).
Self-estimate: roughly 45–55% character accuracy overall. Fixed elements such as the header numbers, "がくもんしよ", "王", "男", "上/下", "いたるまで", "つくりて" and "および" are fairly reliable. Most of the running text is a shape-level guess, which is why there are so many 〔?〕 marks. The hardest parts: the dense hentaigana in the right-page main block (columns B–F, O, P), which I could not parse into meaningful phrases; the faint, gutter-shadowed left-page columns (the first column next to the gutter is almost unreadable and may be partly show-through); the diagonal, smudged bottom-left text block on the left page; the faint caption; and the cut-off 柱 and 丁付 on both outer edges. The cursive form read here as "くに" might be the kanji 國. The "へ/ハ" shape after 王/人 is transcribed as the particle は (hentaigana 八). The combined ▲/△ symbol that starts the right-page bottom block is written as ▲.
