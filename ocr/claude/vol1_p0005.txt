## notes
Summary: The right page (headed 阿蘭陀國 "Holland", with a Chinese "Nanjing man of the Great Qing" standing beside a seated Dutchman reading a book) says the Dutch devote themselves to skills and trade, are versed in astronomy, geography and natural philosophy, collect rare goods from all countries and profit from foreign trade. The left page shows a balloon labelled "Balloon of Washington, United States of North America", and finishes the author's preface: the old red-books' tales (Asahina's island travels, long-legged/long-armed folk, dwarfs) no longer satisfy today's children, so the author changes his wares and offers these "tales of all nations", put into kana from hearsay and scholarship. Signed 辛酉春 (spring 1861) 假名垣魯文記.
Self-estimate: ~85% character accuracy for main kanji/kana text, lower (~65-70%) for furigana. Hardest parts: the furigana on R column 1 (此国/人民/専), the uncertain kanji in L column 3 (春日〔殘?〕 and the character at the column foot partly hidden by the flag drawing), L column 6 井口's reading and the following glyph, the kana run in L column 9, and the seal-script stamp. The balloon band letters are decorative pseudo-Latin that I copied by shape.
