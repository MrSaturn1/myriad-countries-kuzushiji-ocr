## notes
The (Peter the Great) story continues: he was given the titles "Emperor of Russia" and "the Great" (ごらうで), on 22 October 1721 held a peace festival and proclaimed the imperial title, which Prussia, Holland and Sweden recognised first and other countries followed; he reformed customs, punished wrongdoers, built ships and harbours, set trade treaties, in May 1724 crowned his consort Katarina (かたりな) empress, in November married his daughter Anna (あんな) to a "king of Sweden", and in 1724 felt his strength failing from illness. Unsure: furigana on 位爵 and 定 are small; the △/▲ marks differ in fill.
