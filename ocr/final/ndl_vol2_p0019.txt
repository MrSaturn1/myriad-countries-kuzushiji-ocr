## notes
On American industry and government: lacking know-how, America brings in craftsmen from other countries to teach its people, uses water, fire and animal power; cotton cloth is woven by water power and by girls, one-fifth kept at home and the rest sold abroad; woolen cloth, pottery (inferior to China's), books printed with copper plates. In the capital of the United States there are offices with one president (とうりやう) and one vice-president each; officials deliberate together and decide by majority; a single great president and a vice-president are chosen by popular election, must be 35 or older and native-born, and serve four-year terms. Margin page numbers read from the Waseda copy (same spread); unsure: 〔ほか?〕は at the head of R col. 7, 官〔府?〕.
