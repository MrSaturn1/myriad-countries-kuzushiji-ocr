## notes
Opening of part 3: the world is round like an egg and peopled everywhere, and the new world America lies sole-to-sole (antipodal) with Japan; its discovery dates to the reign of Emperor Go-Tsuchimikado, when Columbus (閣龍), a skilled navigator in Spain (いすはにや), judged from the sea winds that a world lay to the west and sought to open it; captions name Queen Isabella (いさべるら) of Spain, civil/military officials and Columbus. Unsure: the emperor ordinal after ⊠ (reads like 三十六代; Go-Tsuchimikado is traditionally the 103rd), the word before のおんときに, the place/word after きやへぶんの (〔具?〕 may be the hentaigana ぐ) and the start of the next column, 造化〔ぎ?〕, 海上〔せう?〕, and 〔くう?〕だい (perhaps くわうだい "vast").
