## notes
Continues London: merchant ships crowd the river mouth so the water looks like dry land; the city's population is about 1,050,000, with tens of thousands of students at the great schools; the English are clever and far-sighted, build great ships and trade with all countries — 28,080 merchant ships, 185,000 men working aboard, 800 royal ships, all flying a cross in red, blue and white, red being most honoured. Unsure: つくり〔て〕 (both tools read そ), ふねのな〔か〕に, and 一百れい五まん (read as 1,050,000).
