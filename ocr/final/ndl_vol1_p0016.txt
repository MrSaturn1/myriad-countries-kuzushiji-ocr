## notes
Peter, gifted beyond other men, resolves to build great ships to cross mountains, rivers and seas, to open relations with learned countries; he makes a Dutch (おらんだ) shipwright master builder, sails to Archangel (あるかんげる) to begin trade, quickly refines his people's rough customs, and tells his ministers that Russia, though in Europe (ゑうらつぱ), should, like other countries… Caption: the Russian emperor opens his treasury and views an old English (英吉利) ship. Unsure: the kanji read as 直 in "〔直?〕し"; page number cut by the binding.
