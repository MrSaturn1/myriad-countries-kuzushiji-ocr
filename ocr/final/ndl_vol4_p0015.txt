## notes
The serpent faces the eagle on the treetop; あゝだむす (Adams, 阿丹土) leaps from hiding with the sword hung on his waist by the immortal's 霊樹 and slashes the serpent from eye to snout; it breathes fire and lunges, the eagle pecks and claws it, and Adams hacks it until it falls dead into the valley as the eagle soars away; picture shows the immortal (仙女) and Adams. Unsure: ねらへど〔ぢん?〕つう (both OCRs がうつう; likely 神通自在), the faint short column 〔そこへ?〕, おろち〔が?〕, and the start of the △ block (しんたい).
