## notes
Columbus (ころん), having won the approval of the King of Hispania (いすはにや), builds a great ship, stocks it with provisions and sails west from the port of Palos (はろす) on 3rd August of Meiō 2; after 34 days of seeing nothing but ocean, the crew turns on him, complaining he has wasted the king's money and they have families at home, and threaten to drown him or beat him to death unless land is found within three days. Unsure: the third column (glyph looks like さ, read as た of 思ひたち?), 王〔家?〕, the column after ひやうらう (〔ろよろ?〕を, perhaps よろづ), 〔むし?〕 (perhaps むき, compass "heading"), 日〔を?〕へて, the column after 五たい (を〔つ?〕 ぼうにて, perhaps a club/rod), and the last faint column before つゞく; continuation marks drawn as ◀/⊗/⊠/● are transcribed as they appear.
