## notes
Franklin is dragged into Adams's compound by young men after calling out; Adams receives him, Franklin apologises and Adams recognises the famous Franklin; Franklin says he trained only in martial arts, and Adams, having heard of his strength, asks for a demonstration, so Franklin lifts a cannon and loads it, aiming at a pointed rock in a lake a ri to the south. The figure is captioned 話聖東 (Washington) although the text names あゝだむす. Unsure: やらんと〔なた?〕へ; the small glyphs after 文武 and 学 (ruby or text?); 千〔斤?〕; 〔其中?〕に; the ゜-like mark in きゝたる。ういるり (dropped); the R bottom block ends まみへ with no visible mark before L ⊠つね〴〵.
