## notes
Right page is the publisher 辻岡屋文助 (金松堂)'s new-spring catalogue for Ansei 7 (1860), listing gōkan titles with authors (為永瓢長, 柳水亭種清, 笠文京, 半文字英寿, 為永千章) and artists (國貞, 國芳, 芳宗, 國盛); left page is the title page of 童絵解万国噺 part 4, vol. 1 (假名垣魯文 trans., 芳虎 ill., 錦橋堂 block) decorated with Western objects labelled lamp (トウラウ, アンドウ), camera (シヤシンキヤフ) and matches (ツケギ). Unsure: the small volume notes on the first entry (初ゟ八迠尾?), several title furigana omitted; library seals ignored.
