## notes
Continuation on America's natural resources: Pennsylvania (へんせいふあにい) coal, once unused because the land was wide and people few; salt from the shore and from a salt pond and salt rocks in the mountains; a mountain whose spring water carries oil (petroleum) used as medicine and for lamps and cooking; gold, silver, copper, coal and salt free for anyone to take; about 130 species of trees, some 3 to 9 jō tall, excellent for ships and masts. Pictures of a wild horse, dog, sheep, wild boar, wild ox, rashamen sheep and a pig. Unsure: the last top column (う〔るし?〕…) and most of the small bottom block (worn in both scans; read from the Waseda copy, same spread); きん〔ぎん?〕; ほは〔じ?〕ら (帆柱?); 野馬/野牛 rubies not read.
