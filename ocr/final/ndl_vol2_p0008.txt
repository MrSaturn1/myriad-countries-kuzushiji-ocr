## notes
R: portrait "A Beauty of the United States" under a banner of decorative pseudo-Latin letters. L: "North America, United States section" — the US is a newly opened country, 1/21 of the world; Italians, French, Spaniards, English and Dutch came to farm the ownerless land; after a famine, the English obtained their queen's leave and first settled Virginia (ひるぎにや), set up a fort and seven officials, then 300 settlers named the land New England (しんいぎりす), followed by 3,000 more; continues "over 200 years ago the Dutch…". The つゞく box is cramped (confirmed by the Waseda copy, whose L page matches; its R page is a different picture); 自立 is followed by a squeezed じりつ, taken as ruby; いぎりすこく and 女王 ruby are faint.
