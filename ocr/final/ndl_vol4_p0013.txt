## notes
A mountain immortal (仙女) tells あゝだむす (Adams, 阿丹土) that his mother was swallowed by a thousand-year-old serpent living in a cave far to the west, summons a white-feathered eagle to guide him there, and gives him a branch of the "テレイブボーム" tree as protection; picture shows Adams and his mother (阿丹土母). Unsure: the kanji after 神仙 (Lite 乱), 大地震, row "そなふるゆへを/ゆへをもて" (possible repeat), the faint short columns after テレイブボーム (ぢゆの えださ ゑあり), and the furigana on 霊鷲 (not read); half-cut page numbers.
