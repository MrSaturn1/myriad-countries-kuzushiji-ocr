## notes
After three years Paulina (はりな) bears a son whom the couple name George Washington (わしんとん げをるげ), "Washington" being the family name; by seven he is precociously brilliant, and he studies astronomy and geography at a school in Williamsburg (〔りあ〕むびゆるぐ). The R-bottom block is worn (checked against the Waseda copy); りちゝの〔はかへ〕, したがひて〔もろい〕 / 〔りあ〕むびゆるぐ, もんじよにいつ, 〔わが〕さとに and the L page number are uncertain; ⊗ = printed crossed-circle mark.
