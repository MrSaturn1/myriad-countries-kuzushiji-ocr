## order
R (つゞき) → L → つぎへ
## notes
Dutch and other settlers swell the population past 20 million; the continent is described in three parts — the Atlantic seaboard, the mountain ridge running north–south, and the land west to the Pacific — with its plains, rivers and waterfalls, and a cold north versus a hot, flood-prone south; picture of fur-clad hunters in a snowy forest. Unsure: 「く〔つ〕み」 (R col 12), 「きたみな〔み〕はまべ」, 「そうた〔い〕」, and the last line 「でき〔ふでき〕かうの」 (lite read できはじ).
