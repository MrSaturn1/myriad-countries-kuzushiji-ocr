## notes
Start of the England section: Britain as a great island west of Europe with Scotland ("すこしや") to the north, united with Ireland under Queen Anne as Great Britain, divided into twelve countries with 62 lords (だいめう), separated from Holland and France by the Calais strait (12–13 ri, a day's sail), and ruled by kings or queens—currently a queen. Page numbers 六/七 and the left 柱 are clearer in the Waseda copy. The 王 after いぎりすの女 and in 女の〔王〕もあり is written like モ/エ (lite read も and こと); だいめう is under a smudge, read with the Waseda copy.
