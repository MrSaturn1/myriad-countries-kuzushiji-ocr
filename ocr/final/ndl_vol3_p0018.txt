## notes
Washington, at 18, organises village boys into drill; a monster in a cave on Mount かくまく east of Virginia (2 jo tall, two heads, five eyes, iron-hard green body, breathing poisonous blue smoke that has killed hundreds of villagers and the hunters who shot at it) — Washington, telling only his parents he is going hunting, takes a musket to the mountain and at night sees glittering lights and blue smoke.
