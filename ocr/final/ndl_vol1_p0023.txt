## order
R top 〔つゞき〕 → L top-right short columns (above the clouds) → L top ○かくて… ▲ → L middle ▲ … △ → R bottom △ → L bottom (めでたし〳〵, end of the Russia story)
## notes
End of the Russia episode: the crown prince (Alexei), stubborn and unfit to rule, fled the country; his father the emperor, enraged, had him hunted down, tortured and executed; Empress Katarina succeeded, extended Russian power over Europe, enriched the people and trade, and passed the throne to the next prince べいとる (printed 三世), under whom the kings of Europe submitted and Russia became an empire equal to "ours" — めでたし〳〵. Caption: "The Russian empress ascends the throne." Unsure: the first two short columns at the L top gutter (faint, 〔いで?〕〔て?〕); 太子《たいし》 furigana read from small marks; ゐせん, なりとくかを as in Lite; ていこく printed with a と-like こ; page numbers cut off.
