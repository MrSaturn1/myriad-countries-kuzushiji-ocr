## order

## notes
France (Paris on the Seine; Bourbon house, republic, Napoleon's empire, restoration of the kingdom by the allied powers) and North America (a European-settled land, capital Washington, trading from the port of California). "ボウルボレ" is as printed (= Bourbon, ン possibly cut like レ); "家産すといへり" ending is worn (す/を and い/ら shapes); left-margin 柱 is mostly cut by the binding; furigana readings are approximate.
