## notes
Front matter of vol. 2: the right page is the publisher's 1860 (Ansei 7) new-release catalogue (Tamenaga Shunsui, Kunisada, Ryūsuitei Tanekiyo, Utagawa Kunisato, Ryūentei Tanehisa, Yoshiiku, Kakutei Shūga) with medicine ads and the publisher Tsujiokaya Bunsuke (Kinshōdō); the left page is the title page of part 2, vol. 1 (Kanagaki Robun, Yoshitora, Kinkyōdō), decorated with a harp and flute. Unsure: 新版 (版/板), the 大貝 price, the small "■■白" note, 盃/孟 in the artist name, and 壽 before 梓.
