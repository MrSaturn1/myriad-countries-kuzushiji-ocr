## notes
America continued: temperance pledges (names recorded in a register; sake shops changing trade after 75 doctors were chosen to study sake's harm), relief for poor sailors' families, the 勧和会 (kankwakai) peace society that reconciles quarrels, the creation myth (上帝 makes a first man as master of all people, then a woman, beginning marriage), monogamy, no child betrothals, courtship for three to five years with family consent, and the wedding ceremony. Page numbers 十八/十九 are read from the Waseda copy (NDL margin cut); unsure: the small mark after 水主 (lite 公, possibly furigana), the glyph after 上帝 (師?), ばんみん, the opening of the ◀ column (only つ…clear), and ゆいのう〔もくろく?〕 (lite むころり).
