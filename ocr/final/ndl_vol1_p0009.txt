## notes
Continuation of the France section: Paris industry—gunsmiths at the bellows, shipyards on the Seine where thousands of shipwrights build and haul great ships, countless craftsmen (clockmakers, tailors, coopers, carpenters, roofers, plasterers, shoemakers, hatters), tribute of metals, coral, crystal and produce from France's islands, and foreign trading ships unloading at Paris, so the land prospers. Order is from the marks and sense: the ⊗ after とぞ and the ⊗ before あまた look identical; the L-middle block ends in a △-like mark (taken to lead to ⊗あまた), and いつ|さいの (一切の) joins R middle to L middle with no mark. Unsure: いへとみ (worn), the circled numeral above the left page.
