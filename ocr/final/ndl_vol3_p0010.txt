## notes
At eighteen Meriken (めりけん, labelled 米理幹) is summoned to serve the English queen; at twenty he is the handsomest man in England and all the court ladies are drawn to him, above all the queen's maid Harina (はりな, 巴利那), a peerless beauty who longs to approach him but is kept off by the eyes of others, until one summer the queen's treasured night-shining jewel is to be aired and Meriken is assigned to guard it (the figure labelled 布魯礼 appears on the left page). Unsure: the small glyph after 国王 (■; looks like a small 弓う with dakuten), the small furigana beside 光 (夜光, read only as やく…), 〔か?〕たち (glyph looks like お), the R-bottom glyph read as 国 (Lite くに), ね in かはらねこひ (print shows ね, sense wants ぬ), and the last column ま〔ぞ?〕や.
