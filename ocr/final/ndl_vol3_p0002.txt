## notes
Front matter of part 3, vol. 1: right page is the publisher Kinshōdō/Tsujiokaya Bunsuke's list of new books for Ansei 7 (1860) plus ads for Takeda plaster (Iijima Keizaburō, Edo Ningyōchō) and Ikkaku-gan pills (Ōno Denbei, Tōgane, Kazusa); left is the title page of 童絵解万国噺 三編上之巻, translated by Kanagaki Robun, illustrated by Ichimōsai Yoshitora, newly carved spring 1861. Unsure: small volume markers before 哥川國郷/after 皐月雨, a furigana kana in 金花七變化, the price 百〔文?〕, and 〔白?〕 beside 金松堂.
