## notes
Russia (R): spanning Europe and Asia, capital 伯徳ろ (Petersburg); the restoring founder-emperor (アレキハ — the short katakana column, perhaps an attempted name) was talented and greatly enlarged the realm, and after his death his consort Catherine (加太里那) took the throne and the country flourished further. England (L): a large island nation west of Europe; under Queen Anne (益那) it united Scotland (思可齋亜) and Ireland (意而蘭土) as Great Britain (大蒲利丹尼亜), capital London (囒頓) on the Thames, divided into 52 counties. Unsure of the first character of the Thames name (〔爹?〕, OCR 多) and the first furigana kana on 大蒲利丹尼亜; ALT_SCAN not needed.
