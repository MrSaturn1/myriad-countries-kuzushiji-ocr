## order
R fans: 山城 (top) → 紀伊 (top right) → 摂津 (middle right) → 武蔵 (middle left) → 陸奥 (bottom left) → 近江 (bottom right; its label 近江玉川 is printed at the left end of the fan, after the poem)
## notes
Endpaper of the Waseda (山田屋) issue: six fan-shaped panels with the classic "Six Jewel Rivers" (六玉川) poems, each labelled with its province: Yamashiro (井出), Kii (高野), Settsu, Musashi, Mutsu (野田) and Ōmi (野路). Uncertain cursive spots: 止/そ in the Yamashiro poem, 手・調布 and the long stroke read as 〳〵 in the Musashi poem, 潮 in the Mutsu poem; the end of the Ōmi poem (after 萩うゑ, and the column after 月や) is unclear. The handwritten shelfmark "A380 (1)" in the top corner is a library mark and is not transcribed.
