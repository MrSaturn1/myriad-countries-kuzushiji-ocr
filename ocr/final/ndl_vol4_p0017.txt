## notes
After the serpent's corpse is burned, あゝだむす (Adams, 阿丹土) returns to the village headman's (せうくわん) house; the widowed headman, impressed by Adams's filial piety, has a sixteen-year-old daughter of fine looks and accomplishments, and wants to take a worthy son-in-law and retire, and begins to favour Adams; picture shows Adams stabbing the serpent coiled round the eagle. Unsure: first character after つゞき (〔ろ?〕し, possibly the end of ころし), and the katakana マヂサハ (read as printed; meaning unclear — possibly a name).
