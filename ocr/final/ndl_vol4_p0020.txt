## notes
Reflecting that the 13 colonies are ruled by officials sent from London and lie open to foreign aggression, Adams (あゝだむす, captioned 阿丹土) drills the village youths in Majisa's (マヂサ) estate with cannon, bombs and bayonet muskets, until William "Hiyuruku" Franklin (ういるりあむ ひゆるくの ふらんきりん, captioned 拂郎基林), passing by on business, stops to watch the drill over the wall. The R-bottom block is badly unclear (〔ろう?〕じ, 〔ま?〕うと, 〔が?〕とし, the stray ざ/わ glyphs, 〔はへたる?〕, かのん〔をも?〕ちゐる); also unsure: 兵〔法?〕, 〔な?〕んどの, 兵器〔など?〕, R page number (十六 in the Waseda copy); マチサ is printed without dakuten in one place.
