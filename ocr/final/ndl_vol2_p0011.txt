## notes
England's king imposes heavy taxes on America; the Americans, resenting his greed, swear an oath not to obey, so the king sends warships that burn houses and seize goods; the leaders (Adams, 阿丹土 あだむす, pictured) secretly gather strong men, raise ships and make Washington (華盛頓 わしんとん, pictured) commander, then circulate a manifesto to the thirteen colonies saying that Heaven gave people life and their lot and that the strong must not oppress the weak. Unsure: the bottom-right manifesto opening (いわくでん / たうきみを / せうじだん / ぞく / あつ, plus a stray vertical stroke after it), the reading はらから for the short columns before きやうだい, 〔う〕ん上 (年貢運上?), and the inscriptions and pseudo-Roman letters drawn in the picture, which were not transcribed.
