## notes
The demon, ordered to go east, rows west instead, covering 3,500 ri in half an hour, lands the ship on an island of America and vanishes; Merriken (めりけん) and Paulina (はりな/巴利那) revive the crew, the convicts land at Virginia (ひるぎにや) in English territory, and Merriken settles in Virginia (Fairfax?), clears new land, has the convicts open gold mines at Mexico (めきしこ) and becomes the New World's richest man; it says this happened in Japan's Kyōhō era. Washington (話聖東) appears as a picture label. Uncertain: the speech balloons, とき〔ほど〕の, the place-name column にや部内〔郡〕〔ふわいる〕/あきす, わが〔てう〕, and the first glyph of 〔き〕つけくすり; ◬ ⊗ ⊠ ◀ ◉ stand for the printed continuation marks.
