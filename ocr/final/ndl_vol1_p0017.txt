## order

## notes
Russia lacks learning, so Peter invites scholars; the sage Lefort (れほる) of Geneva (ぜねへる, said to be in へるしや) becomes his friend and urges trade; ships are sent via the Baltic (おほすと海), Black Sea and Russia's great rivers; in 1697 Peter travels abroad, leaves government to three officials, goes to Holland (おらんだ) in Dutch clothes under the name Pyotr Mikhailov (べいとるみかえろふ), lives disguised as a workman at Zaandam (さんだん), corresponds with his ministers, fells timber himself, and returns to Amsterdam (あむすてるだむ). Unsure: the bottom-right of R is torn in the NDL copy and was read from the Waseda copy; "ふうぞくを見んと / しよこくの / ふう〔を?〕 / 見ると" and "い / ふく / 〔を?〕 / ちや / くし" (= 衣服を着し?) are uncertain; "みかどいふ" and "しよくをつくる" read as printed but sense unclear.
