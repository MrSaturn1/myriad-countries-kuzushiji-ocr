## notes
In September (1724) the emperor (Peter the Great) visited a place called すてるべき and an iron(?)-works, then at らくだ (Lakhta) saw a boat of his men about to capsize in rough water, leapt into the water and rescued 24 of them; his illness returned, and on 8 February he died aged 52 amid universal mourning; Empress Katarina (かたりな) succeeded him and governed well. Caption: "The Russian emperor saves his soldiers in the water." Unsure: しかぢいもの (agreed by both tools, meaning unclear); かぜ dakuten; furigana on the caption partly read from the Waseda copy; page numbers cut off.
