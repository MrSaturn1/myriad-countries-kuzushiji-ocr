## notes
The Americans' declaration: a ruler is set up to protect the people, not to oppress them; America had no chiefs until the English came and made themselves king, and despite patient endurance and repeated pleas from the elders the English king never mended his ways, so the colonies listed his wrongs in twelve articles, made known to all lands that they would found their own country and choose their own commander. The English king, enraged that the thirteen colonies of the New World (しんせかい) had united, sent more warships; after a year of inconclusive war France (ふらんすこく) sent troops to help America, and after six or seven years of fighting the English king, unable to win, sought peace, and the American elders deliberated. Unsure: いでゝわが〔つを〕 (expected つみ "our fault"), しらしむりと, 各国〔部〕, and たち〔さち〕さう before だんして (expected さうだん "deliberation").
