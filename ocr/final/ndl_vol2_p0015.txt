## notes
America's presidents serve four-year terms: Washington (8 years, honoured as 国父 "father of the country"), then Adams (あゝだむす), Jefferson (ゑつひるそん), Madison (まぢそん), J. Q. Adams (あだむしす) and Van Buren (ばんびゆれん); the country grows from 13 to 26 states (Vermont ふるまんど, Ohio, Louisiana, Indiana, Mississippi, Illinois, Missouri, Michigan, Arkansas…), population over 13 million, settled by Europeans (Italy, France, Holland, England, Spain, Sweden) rather than Chinese/Japanese/Siamese/Vietnamese, mostly English in dress and manners; caption: picture of a steamship. Unsure: glyph after 国父 (〔殿?〕), first state name after △ (〔ゑ?〕にやす), the kanji after 二の巻より in the box, and R bottom 「つ〔たへ〕り」; L top has two printed round marks (a seal-like 〔樽?〕 and ㊂).
