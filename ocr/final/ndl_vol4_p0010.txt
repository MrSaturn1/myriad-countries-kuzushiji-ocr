## notes
Franboresu has the monster's body sent to be burned and leads Washington to his home at "Uirri-hiyuruku", where his wife serves a Western meal (chicken, pork/beef?, milk, wine) and Washington relaxes; meanwhile the villagers find the two-headed monster's corpse in a ravine at Kakumakuzan, but it is iron-hard and will not burn, so they bury it under an uprooted tree and return to "Uirugu-hiyurugu". Labels: カレル, 老母, ワシントン, フランボレス, フランキリン. Unsure: こうし/ころし (row 19), たをれ/たはれ, さては, てがけ/とがけ; ⊠ is a boxed-X continuation mark; the box つぎのゑとき sits between the two L-top sections.
