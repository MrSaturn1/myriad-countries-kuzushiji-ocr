## notes
Franklin's cannon shot shatters the rock, astonishing Adams and all; Franklin says his strength is nothing beside his friend Washington (わしんとん げをるげ) of Virginia (ひるきにや), a young man of letters and arms who killed a monster at かくまくざん and, when a great tiger killed his servant at the foot of 大らう山, kicked, wrestled and beat it to death with his fists; Adams asks Franklin to introduce him to Washington, Franklin agrees, and he is feasted. Labels: 阿丹土 (Adams), 拂郎基林 (Franklin), ヱジせ (woman at the window, unclear). Unsure: the first glyph after ▲ (〔とら?〕), 〔虎?〕, あし〔を?〕, づ〔さ?〕 (従者), 〔ぼ?〕んにん, しるさしたる, and both page numbers (R cut away, L shows only 二).
