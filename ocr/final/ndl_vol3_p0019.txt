## notes
Washington aims at the glittering lights and fires twice; the mountain roars, a great tree snaps at the root, and he believes he has killed the monster, but fearing its poisonous smoke he waits for dawn, goes down toward the village to check, and sees firelight that he takes for a house where he can spend the night. Unsure: ねらひう〔た〕んと, 青烟〔気〕 (third glyph has small side kana), the final words of the R bottom block (read as かしこにいたり〔て〕), and the faded end of the far-left block's 6th column (くつのまゝ? / あしを?) and start of the 7th; page numbers mostly cut.
