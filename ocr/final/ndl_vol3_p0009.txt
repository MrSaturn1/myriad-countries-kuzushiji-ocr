## notes
Columbus (ころん) lands on what is now Colombia (ころんびや); in Eishō 5 the Portuguese king's vassal Americus (あめりきゆす) sails out hoping to equal Columbus, opens lands south near New Granada (がらなだ), and so the New World north and south is called America; 300 years later Washington (わしんとん) founds an independent nation in North America — his father, of English stock and called Meriken (めりけん), descended from Americus, moved from Portugal to England, and Meriken was brilliant from childhood and devoted to the martial arts and natural philosophy. Unsure: the mark before わしんとん (〔一?〕), 去 in くにを去いぎりす (could be さ), おさなき (first glyph あ/お), the furigana on 独立, and second あめりきゆす (top-left col) read as ゆ like the later one.
