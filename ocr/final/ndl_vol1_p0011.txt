## order
R top つゞき ▲ → R middle (beside the standing figure) ▲ … △ → L bottom △ 次へ; caption runs R bottom → L bottom right (英吉利の女王諸侯の礼を請くる図)
## notes
After taking the throne, the queen of England marries two princes of a vassal-state king and governs with her two consorts standing at her right and left; when the daimyo (lords) come to court they approach and kneel before the queen. Caption: "The Queen of England receiving the homage of her lords." Unsure: 〔むすこ〕 (cursive ligature), the furigana/okurigana of 請, and the margin 柱/leaf numbers (cut at the binding).
