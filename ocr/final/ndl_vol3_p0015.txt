## notes
A storm strikes the ship and a three-headed, six-eyed demon seizes the mast; the crew faint, but Merriken (めりけん) and his wife stay calm, and the demon, calling itself a demon of some island, offers to steer the ship wherever he names; knowing it does the opposite, Merriken says "west" so it rows the ship east. The demon's speech on L (〽われは… / ◀ある… / 仙人〔様〕…) is crowded around a rope and its column order and the 仙人 column are uncertain; 〔こつ〕ぜん is a cursive form (probably 忽然); ◬/◭ = triangle continuation marks of two different shapes, ⊠ = boxed mark; R page number cut by the binding.
