## notes
Heat and cold vary with the land; of all things people need, gold and silver (きん〴〵) are the most essential, then copper, iron and tin; gold and silver were once scarce in America, but recently mines were found in North Carolina (北かろりな) and elsewhere, yielding much gold, silver, copper and mercury (みつかね) each year; caption: miners digging gold and silver in the mountains of California (加洲), U.S. territory. Unsure: R 「あつ〔き〕さむさ」 (lite read あつささむさ), the end of the last L line before つゞく, and the badly worn caption (the kanji before 連 and most of its furigana, also checked on the Waseda copy).
