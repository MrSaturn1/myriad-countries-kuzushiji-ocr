## notes
Kanagaki Robun's preface to part 3 (spring 1861): this is a children's version, retold in plain Japanese kana, of accounts of the new country (America) drawn from the Chinese 海国図志 (based on the Dutchman 葛拉墨兒's original), 増訳采覧異言, 亜墨利加一統志 and 西洋襍記; now in its third part, rushed out by the publisher. Several furigana are read from small worn kana (e.g. 葛拉墨兒《うるめろし》); the margin page number and the seal beside the roundel 一 are uncertain.
