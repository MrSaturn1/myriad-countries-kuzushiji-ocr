## notes
Majisa (マヂサ), moved by Adams (あゝだむす), offers him his house and his daughter; Adams demurs as a lowly peasant whom the villagers might not obey, and the next day Majisa summons the people of his 36 villages to announce he will make Adams his son-in-law and heir to his office.
