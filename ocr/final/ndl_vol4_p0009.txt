## order
R top つゞき … ▲ → R bottom ▲ → L bottom つゞく; labels on figures
## notes
Franklin, his sister and Washington set off; Franklin recognises their father Franboresu leading 14–15 armed villagers searching for her, and Franklin tells him of the sister's rescue by Washington and of Washington's slaying of the monster at "Kakumakuzan"; the father thanks Washington warmly. Labels name 加礼留 (Franklin's sister), 拂郎基林 (Franklin), 話聖東 (Washington), ふらんぼれす. Unsure: ちかづくまゝに (row 7), the 2–3 faint characters after ▲き in the R bottom block, and the last words before つゞく (うちとめ〔け?〕しといへる).
