## notes
Washington rescues Franklin's sister, who had been bound by two ruffians before a roadside shrine; she thanks him as her life-saver, Franklin marvels at the young Washington who had already slain the monster of "Kakumakuzan" and destroyed bandits, and the grateful brother and sister are invited to rest at his home in "Uirriamu-hiyuruku" (Williamsburg?) and reunite with their father Franboresu, shown in the picture searching for his daughter with villagers. Unsure: きくたびに (row 42), the とく〳〵 before ト, final はらから; page numbers 四/五 read from the Waseda copy (faint in NDL).
