## notes
Washington (話聖東/わしんとん), sword drawn, is confronted by a young man; Carol (加礼留/かれる) cries out that he is her brother, so Washington realises he is no bandit and sheathes; the youth sits down and introduces himself as Franklin (拂郎基林/ふらんきりん), son of the farmer Franklin-bores (ふらんぼれす) of Virginia (ひるぎにや), explaining he had been keeping vigil at a shrine, heard a disturbance, saw Washington apparently killing someone, and took him for a bandit. Unsure: the weapon word はう〔じぐい?〕 (perhaps ぼうちぎり "staff"), small glyphs after 部内, まいよ〔この社に?〕, and the faint end of the R-bottom block (Waseda copy checked; same spread).
