## order
L top picture (no text) → zodiac grid, columns right to left: 十干 甲–戊, 己–癸, then 十二支 子–巳, 午–亥 → publisher line (南傳馬丁二丁目 small, 山田屋庄次郎板) at far left
## notes
Colophon page of the Waseda (山田屋) issue: a grid of small pictures labelling the ten heavenly stems and twelve zodiac branches with their kana readings, and the imprint "published by Yamadaya Shōjirō, Edo picture-book and nishiki-e wholesaler, Minami-Denmachō 2-chōme". The furigana of 亥 is a hentaigana read as ゐ; the library barcode sticker is not transcribed, and the 魯文譯 / 芳虎画圖 cartouche at the gutter belongs to the right page (other job).
