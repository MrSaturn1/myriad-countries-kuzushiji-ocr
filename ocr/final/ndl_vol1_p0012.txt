## notes
Continues the court etiquette of England (a lord presents his palm to the queen, who presses it with her finger, then he withdraws), then describes the capital London on the Thames: houses packed like comb teeth, a great bridge 180 jō long with three lamp platforms, cannon batteries on the banks, and several trading places where England's goods (wool cloth, grain, tin, lead, iron, hemp) are traded with merchants of all nations. Unsure: the verb in しよこうその手を〔かき〕て, dakuten on さしい〔だ〕, and the cut page number.
