## notes
Columbus (ころん) smiles at his mutinous crew, tells them he knows by his learning that a world lies to the west and promises they will see it within three days; he orders the officer Escobedo (ゑすこへとる) up the mast, who sights an island to the south-west through a telescope; the crew rejoice and apologise, Columbus forgives them, lands and names the island San Salvador (〔さ?〕んとさるはとる), an island south of North America; he then sails south, finds Haiti (ハイチ), returns to report the New World to the king, often travels back and forth to Haiti, and in Eishō 3 (1506) under Emperor Go-Kashiwabara (後柏原) first reaches the mainland (つぎへ → NDL vol3 p0009 "じやうりくすることをえたり"). The only scan of this leaf; uncertain spots: 〔先?〕王 (single kanji before 王, OCR あん/おん); てんが〔く?〕 (OCR てんがんつか; a ろ/う-like glyph); 〔吾?〕 twice (a コ+旦-like glyph that both OCRs read われ); 〔さ?〕んとさる (OCRs read し, but the glyph looks like さ); あめりか〔ふね?〕で〔た?〕こく (the glyph after ふ is not the ね used in めがね; it may be ろ (呂), so the text might mean Florida (ふろりだこく)); 〔きは?〕 after 柏原 (a kanji-like glyph and a small ハ); 永正〔寅?〕; the R box numeral reads 一 (the top stroke is the box frame), which agrees with the L box 一の巻より. The ⊠ marks stand for the printed boxed-cross continuation marks. Page numbers 五/六 are half cut by the binding (六 is unsure). The ○三 is a circled 三 at the top of the left page.
