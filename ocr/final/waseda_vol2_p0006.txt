## notes
Corrected after the Part 3 translation pass: the first cartouche reads 話聖東妻 加礼留 ("Caroline, Washington's wife") in both copies (cf. NDL vol3 p0006), not 〔諸?〕国 東夷.
Character-list picture page: three name cartouches label figures in a battle scene — Kareru (加礼留, an "eastern barbarian" from some country), Ashura (阿修羅), an English official, and Washington (話聖東), "son of America" (米理幹男 read めりけんせがれ). Unsure: the small first character of the 加礼留 cartouche (read 諸?), small kana beside 加礼留 (not transcribed), and the margin 柱 and page number, of which only the edges show at the binding.
