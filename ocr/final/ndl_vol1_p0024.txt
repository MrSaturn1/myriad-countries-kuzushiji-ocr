## notes
End of vol. 1: a notice that part 2 will cover Holland, America and Nanjing, with a portrait of the Russian Empress and a view of St Petersburg (伯徳琭), credits to Kanagaki Robun (text) and Ichimosai Yoshitora (pictures); the left page is publisher's ads (tooth powder, whitening powder 白妙, bedwetting cure) and a book catalogue (Kyozan, Hiroshige, Kunisada, Tanekiyo). Unsure: col 4 "つ候間" (り apparently omitted), the end of the 白妙 ad, the first glyph of the bedwetting ad's 2nd line, the small line beside 雛鶴笹湯寿, the 〔狂?〕言 prefix, and the faint margin page number.
