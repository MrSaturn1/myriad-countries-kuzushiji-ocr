## notes
Washington's descendants proving unwise, the elders deliberate, choose a wise man as head with a four-year term, settle the civil and military offices, and build the capital on 35 ri of land by the Potomac (ほとまく) between Virginia (ゐるぎにや) and Maryland (まれいらんど); with laws in place, trade with Europe and Asia makes America a prosperous new nation. Unsure: R top 「に〔日?〕」, L top 「くにの〔を〕〔さ〕となし」 (glyphs read as をさ? = head), R bottom col 2–3 end 「又〔ゐる?〕ぎや」 and 「さう〔だん?〕けつ」 (NDL very faint there; read mostly from Waseda copy).
