## notes
Russia: after his father's death, Peter's (べいとる) uncle schemes to rule; at 18 Peter learns of the plot and confines him; the uncle had set Peter's elder brother on the throne so there were two emperors, but Peter deposes his brother and rules all Russia; opening the treasury he sees a great ship obtained from England (いぎりす), admires its construction and resolves to build ships and travel to all countries, but no one in Russia knows shipbuilding and his courtiers despair. Unsure: the columns either side of the gutter at the top (R "みかどがしなん / にてくらゐを", L "ふまへしをごとく") are worn and only partly legible; "かやうせり", "かんしやうなし", "うみ", "あしを" are best guesses.
