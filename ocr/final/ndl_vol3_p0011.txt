## notes
Set to guard the jewel with Harina (はりな) at his side, Meriken (めりけん) receives from her a love letter written in Western script; he answers that he is not unmoved but that the Queen's (女王) charge is heavy and if they are seen both will suffer, so she should leave; Harina protests that English women may be thought immodest, but her love is not mere infatuation — her father descends from Columbus (ころん) of Hispania (いすはにや), who opened the New World, and he has always told her to marry a capable man and cross to America, the land her ancestor opened (figures labelled 布呂礼, 米理幹, 巴利那). Unsure: the first glyph of L top (〔め?〕だし, perhaps いだし continuing たまづさをさし), どころ (first glyph unclear), the mark sequence ▲へ at the end of the L top block (the R side resumes △うへ), ⊠き〔た?〕 at the start of the L bottom block, and the faint page numbers.
