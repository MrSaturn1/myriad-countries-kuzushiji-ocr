## notes
End of the volume on America: some people sail to all countries to trade; first oppressed by England, the people, through Washington, Franklin and Adams, achieved their aim, and at the 1783 peace in Paris (ぱりす) the United States' independence was recognised, a great deed shining across the world; the next parts (3rd: Holland/Spain, Turkey, 4th and 5th) are announced, and the right page shows a balloon labelled CONSTITUTION with the credits 魯文譯 (translated by Robun) and 芳虎畫圖 (pictures by Yoshitora). The left page is the publisher Tsujiokaya Bunsuke (金松堂, Yokoyama-chō, Edo) catalogue of new books for spring Ansei 7 (1860), with authors 為永瓢長, 柳水亭種清, 花笠文京, 半文字英寿, 為永千章 and artists 國貞, 國芳, 芳宗, 國盛. The Waseda alt matches only the right page (its left page is another publisher's back matter). The NDL main block is smudged: the end of col. 1, ちゆうぎ (possibly ちから), the verb in col. 8, and the furigana in the catalogue (especially 釜ヶ渕水外石川, and the volume/よみ切 notes) are uncertain.
