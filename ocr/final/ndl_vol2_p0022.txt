## notes
Continues the account of America (めりけん): schools with yearly examinations and four-year courses, graduates becoming officials, craftsmen rewarded for new inventions using water and fire power, prisoners taught trades and reformed by reading books on the day of worship, music and drawing schools, schools for the deaf, blind and mute (embossed books), and orphans being raised; pictured are a bridegroom (むこ), bride (よめ) and an officiant (宝師). Uncertain: the short columns under the shoes (read as 〔こ?〕〔ろ?〕〔に?〕つみあるものは), 官人's gloss (くわんじん as printed?), and the badly worn last columns of the left page bottom strip after さけをふかくのみ大き〔に?〕ゑひ (columns from き■ to ■■つと are mostly unreadable in both scans); the ⌂ is a house-shaped continuation mark.
