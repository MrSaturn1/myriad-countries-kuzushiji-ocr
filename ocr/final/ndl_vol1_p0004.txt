## notes
Inner title page of 童繪解萬國噺 初編 (printed "下之巻"; by Kanagaki Robun, pictures by Mōsai Yoshitora, Yamadaya edition) and Robun's preface, dated spring of Man'en 2 (1861): citing Sugawara no Michizane's poem, he says that in this peaceful age one can learn of distant lands without travelling, and that he has culled foreign books and rendered them in easy kana for children and women as a pastime for long days and nights. The ALT_SCAN (Waseda) matches only the left page (its right page is different); a round seal-script mark above the preface frame is not transcribed; furigana are approximate in places.
