## notes
Washington cuts down the two bandits and turns to the maiden, when a masked bandit chief bursts from the shrine and grabs his scabbard; Washington shakes free and fights, calling "Wait, young man, I have something to say", but the chief, a skilled fighter, attacks with a huge stone pole; as dawn breaks his hat falls off revealing a fair, imposing hero of nearly thirty, yet Washington, taking him for a bandit, keeps his guard while the maiden lifts her head to look at the man's face. Unsure: ⊗返 (kanji 返 alone before ふたゝび), りし〔と〕〔て〕, ぞ〔く〕と, こへ〔て〕身の; continuation marks rendered approximately (⊠ used for two different box-hourglass marks).
