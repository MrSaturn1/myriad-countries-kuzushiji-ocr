## notes
Final page of this volume: Adams feasts Franklin, they talk of heroes past and present until sunset, Franklin takes his leave and Adams and his wife see him off; the author then announces that part 5 will tell how Washington, Adams and Franklin swear brotherhood, gather people in Virginia (ひるぎにや) and plan to make the thirteen states of North America independent, with parts 6-8 to follow; credits 仮名垣魯文 (text), 一猛斎芳虎 (pictures), 錦橋堂 (publisher). The left page is an older 和泉屋市兵衛 catalogue dated 天保六年 (1835) listing 金瓶梅, 金毘羅船利生纜 (馬琴), etc., plus cosmetic adverts (美艶仙女香, 黒油美玄香); a library label (207/4/1696) covers the top of the 曲輪達引 and advert columns (■). Unsure: た〔か?〕どの, 千里もが〔つ?〕べき, ふ〔う?〕ふ, page number.
