## notes
Paulina (はりな) recalls her ancestor's dying wish and begs Merriken (めりけん), descendant of Americus (あめりきゆす), to marry her and take her to the New World/America; he agrees and they vow to cross to America, but his wicked colleague(s) Furore (ふろれ) overhear and rejoice at a chance to frame him. ⊗ and ⊠ stand for the printed crossed-circle and boxed-hourglass continuation marks; よりとしわか〔い〕 (or わかく) and the reading of ふろれ / ねいかん are slightly uncertain.
