## notes
Colour frontispiece of part 3 naming characters: Washington (詰聖東), son of Meriken (米理幹), in black coat and sword; Washington's wife Karel(?) (加礼留), the tall lady on R; and an English official named 阿修羅, the scowling man with the sabre on L, while dark-skinned men in blue coats grapple with muskets on R. Furigana read from small worn kana in both copies (Waseda vol2/p0006 used to confirm): 加礼留《かれる》 fairly sure (か written as hentaigana like ゥ); 男《せがれ》, 官人《くわんにん》 and 阿修羅《あしゆら》 uncertain in their middle kana. 詰 (言+吉) is as printed for わしん. Page numbers (lower outer margins) are cut at the binding: two strokes visible on R, possibly three on L.
