## notes
After the King rewards (Admiral Penn's) services with gold, (his son, apparently "いるれむ" = William) declines and asks instead for new land; the King grants a charter and he goes to America, naming the land "ぺんせへるふあにい" (Pennsylvania); other English settlers found Maryland (まれいらんど), Virginia (ひるぎにあ) and Carolina (かろりな), forming the thirteen colonies of about 1.45 million people. The bottom text says each colony had officials appointed from England under English law, the French opened "New France" (しんふらんす) in the north, and the English later drove the French out, after which the land prospered for 40–50 years. Unsure: the glyph after 国王 (cursive kanji glued with furigana とく/こく), the column read みつろ (possibly 其子 "his son"), and the badly worn lower L columns (でんぢ…/…とき/さんにな/其うへ…/よ…).
