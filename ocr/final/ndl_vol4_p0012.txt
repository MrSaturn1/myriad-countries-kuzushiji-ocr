## notes
Young Adams climbs down into a ravine to pick a cherry branch for his crippled mother, returns to find her gone, and searching the deep mountains in grief and rage sees a glittering cloud; approaching it sword drawn he is paralysed, and a lady clad in leaves emerges from a cave, laughs, and tells him she is no monster but the guardian of the land since this New World was opened. Labels on the L picture: 話聖東 (Washington) and 車馬二騎. R-bottom columns 2–5 are badly worn (はせつけ, げんをぬき, わけ入 uncertain); also unsure: まづ (L row 2), むねんく / ぢだんだふみ, and the name spelled あゝだむす throughout (tools split between あだむす/あらだむす/あゝだむす).
