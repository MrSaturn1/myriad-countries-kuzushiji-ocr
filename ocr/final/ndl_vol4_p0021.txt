## notes
Franklin (ふらんきりん, captioned 拂郎基林) watches over the wall as a young man (Adams, captioned 阿丹土) rides out, whirls his spear like lightning, plants it in the ground, then shoots an arrow that pierces its shaft 8–9 sun deep; Franklin cries "well done!" and the youths notice the onlooker; Ejise (ヱジセ) is pictured on the R page. The R bottom run of tiny columns is faint and unsure (う〔し?〕まに, 〔ど?〕うと〔つ?〕きたて, かき〔?〕); also ひらめ〔か?〕し and the page numbers (十七/十八 in the Waseda copy).
