## notes
End of the upper fascicle and start of the lower: the immortal vanishes, the eagle leads あゝだむす (Adams, 阿丹土) about three ri to a great tree over a valley, he hides to wait for the serpent that killed his mother (阿丹土母), and a foul wind and black clouds herald a huge serpent rising from the valley; right page carries the credits 魯文作 / 芳虎画. Hourglass-shaped continuation marks transcribed as ⧗; unsure: 木立〔坂?〕, いち〔ぢん?〕 (both OCRs いちどう), は〔な?〕, and the ruby on 大樹/砂石; top margin has a circled 三 and a small (censor?) seal, page numbers half cut.
