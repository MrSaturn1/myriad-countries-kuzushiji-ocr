## notes
America continued: marriage registered with names sealed and filed in the population register; no change in marriage customs since; in the 26 states (二十六部) of the United States there are no lords and vassals, only ranks of officials (国領・部領・府領・県領), and dress and food do not differ by rank; the family relations resemble China; food (three meals a day, meat of chicken, pig, fish, duck, cow, sheep, mostly roasted, cut with knives instead of chopsticks, fruit, sweets, milk, eggs, tea and sake), travel by carriage and horse, no festivals except worship every seventh day, and burial customs (washing, coffin, stone/iron/tin enclosures, black mourning clothes, gravestones with birth and death dates); caption: "Americans watching a balloon (風船)". The short columns under the feet on the right page are badly worn in NDL and only partly legible in Waseda; 国領/部領 glosses and the small marks beside them, 三倫's gloss and 三五日を〔へ?〕て (both OCRs 入て) are uncertain.
