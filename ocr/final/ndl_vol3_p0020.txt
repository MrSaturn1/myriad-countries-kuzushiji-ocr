## notes
The "house" turns out to be a mountain shrine where fierce bandits have a campfire and a beautiful girl of about sixteen tied to a tree, threatening her with swords; Washington, judging them kidnappers who mean to sell her to a brothel, steps out, kicks one down, throws the other aside, frees the girl, and the bandits rise, threaten to cook him, and attack — he dodges and grabs one by the collar. Labels: 話聖東 (Washington) and 本名未詳 ("real name unknown") beside a masked figure. Unsure: the faded opening columns of the R bottom block (くびすぢつかみ / てだまのごとく are best guesses from both scans), 山神〔焼〕, ふりたて〔ゝ〕.
