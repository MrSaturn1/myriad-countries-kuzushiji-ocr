## notes
(a) Summary: Opening of a new instalment (title cartouches 魯文作 / 芳虎画), continuing the story of Meriken (めりけん) and Harina (はりな). While the two are together, someone breaks into their room and smashes the national treasure, a jewel (めいぎよく), into pieces. Meriken resolves to kill himself for failing to guard it, but Harina stops him, and the loyal Flore (ねつしんふろれ) reports it all to the King. The Queen summons her ministers. Meriken's crime is grave, but he is a descendant of Americus (あめりきゆす) and Harina of Columbus (ころん), so the sentence is softened to exile to Carolina in North America. They leave the Thames (ていむす川) on a convict ship with some 300 others. Off Bermuda (はるまた) a sudden storm blows up (つゞく). Illustrations: an open boat in heavy seas (R), and a large warship with sailing ships (L).
Layout: R top block (つゞき box) → ▲ → R middle block → "三の巻へ" box. L "二の巻より" block → ▲ → middle block (ending ければ△) → △ bottom block → つゞく. The ○めりけんはりなを…しゆつはんなす block beside the ships sits outside the ▲/△ chain, so it is tagged [caption]. 配流《はいる》: the reading is printed small in two half-columns under the kanji, not as side ruby. The 柱 is cut at the fold in both copies: R shows the left halves (万?, 国?, 三) and L, in the Waseda copy only, shows the right halves; page numbers are 十 (R) and 〔十一?〕 (L). The top margin of L has a circled 三 (○三) and the censor seal 酉改 (1861). The NDL copy (vol3/p0013) was confirmed visually to be the same spread and is the main source; Waseda was used to cross-check.
(b) Confidence: about 93% of characters are sure. Of the remaining 7%, most are the 10 〔?〕 items plus dakuten choices and hentaigana. The weakest spots: 国王〔注進?〕に (the glyph after 国王 could also be 訴; OCR guesses are とく / つかに / 野に / とうふ); 〔ふ?〕ちんみづから; 萬死〔ば?〕んに (an odd sequence); ひ〔か?〕されて; こびに〔そ?〕れころん (only one glyph is visible between に and れ; all four OCRs read にてこれ); うかゞひ見〔て?〕 (OCRs read 見つゝ); しゆつ vs しやつ; そのち (printed without ゝ as far as I can see; v3-NDL reads そのゝち). No ■.
(c) Candidates (counts from a char-level diff against this reference, plus inspection):
- NDL v3 on Waseda: good characters but poor block order. Captions (魯文作 / 芳虎画) are spliced into the text, and the ▲/△ jumps are not followed, so the R middle block and the L lower blocks are interleaved. It drops furigana, 〳〵 and the つゞく box. About 27 local misreads: dakuten dropped (ぎ→き, ご→こ, ど→と, だ→た, び→ひ, ぜ→せ), か→つ (うつゞひ), ゞ→ら (とらむる), 見→は, め→は, read 見つゝ, 一ツ→一つ, and some dropped words (なか, みちび). Roughly 60–70 wrong or missing characters, plus about 300 characters out of order.
- NDL v3 on NDL scan: same profile, about 33 local misreads (起流 for 配流, もいじぎよく, □□ for コハい, とく for 注進に, はむす川 for ていむす川), 2 □. It also injects 芳虎画 and orders the left page's three blocks wrongly (せつのたから… / ばつえう… / こそきは… are interleaved). Roughly 70 wrong characters.
- NDL Lite on Waseda: best character accuracy on Waseda, about 20 local misreads, but the block order is jumbled. Fragments are out of sequence (なさけに, いちだいじ…, コハ…); it adds noise lines (刃目三, a hallucinated line この一つのことをもついている。, □□□□□□□, 万) and has 7 □. Errors include 萬→蔦, ゆ→や, かたゝいふ, うつゞひ, ろかゞひ, ふ事 for ふね. Roughly 40–50 wrong characters.
- NDL Lite on NDL scan: the best candidate. About 21 local misreads and only about 47 characters in big blocks (noise lines 刃倒二一, まう, ②, 曲曲, □×7, and the duplicated みたまをはいしあま line). Errors: 萬→蔦, ゆ→や once, け→げ (なさげ), and かぜ→がせ, plus the OCR-wide misreadings (つゝ, てこれ, 野に). Almost all furigana and line order are right. Roughly 30–35 wrong characters.
- Claude by eye: very low accuracy on the dense kana, with an edit distance of about 520 against roughly 910 reference characters. Most kana runs are garbled hentaigana misreads (う/か, ゑ/し, あ/な, ぐ/が …). It does get the kanji, the box labels (つゞき, 二の巻より, 三の巻へ, つゞく), the margin seal and ○三, and several phrases (ひとまのうちより, 三百よにんと, をよせんと). It wrongly adds うばひとつて and also reads てむせ川 for ていむす川. About 50% of characters are wrong.
