## notes
R ends the England section: England holds territory in India (Tenjiku) with 100 large ships and about 4,000 officials, conscripts men aged 18–45, and employs many black servants ("kuronbō") from its colonies who, born in hot lands, swim like fish day and night without tiring; credit "translated by Robun, illustrated by Yoshitora". L begins the Russia (Oroshiya) section: a great country spanning Europe and Asia, formerly called Muscovy (むすこびや), whose emperor Peter (べいとるてい) was born in Moscow (もすこう) in 1672 by Russian reckoning, so wise from childhood that his father passed over the heir and made him king at age 11. Unsure: the word for the black servants (〔ふかついす〕じん; lite ふかついす, v3 and the Waseda copy look more like ふろいす), な〔か〕の, あ〔たか〕も, つかる〔ゝ〕, and the heading's small furigana.
