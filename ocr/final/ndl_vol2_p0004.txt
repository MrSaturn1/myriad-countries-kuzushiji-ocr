## notes
Colour title spread for part 2 of the book, showing a Western man holding a scroll (right) and a Western woman in a crinoline dress and bonnet (left), with credits to Kanagaki Robun (translation), Ichimōsai Yoshitora (pictures) and the publisher Kinkyōdō; the 上/下 roundels mark the upper and lower volumes. Furigana on 童 and 貳 are small and partly uncertain.
