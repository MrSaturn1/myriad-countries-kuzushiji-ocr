## notes
Majisa (マヂサ) has the people of his 36 villages drop ballots into a box on whether Adams (あゝだむす, captioned 阿丹土) should be his son-in-law; all approve, his daughter Ejise (ヱジセ) consents, they marry, and Adams wins the villagers' respect for his decisive management; Franklin is pictured (caption 拂郎基林). Unsure: "み〔て?〕" before ⊗, the last bottom-left words "なかりけり" (faint), the L page number (十六 in the Waseda copy); continuation marks normalised to ▲△⧗⊗⊖.
