## notes
Front cover of vol. 1 (童繪解萬國噺 初編上巻, translated/written by Kanagaki Robun, pictures by Mōsai Yoshitora, published by Kinkyōdō) with its facing page of publisher's ads for tooth/gum medicine (固齦散), a whitening wash-powder (白妙), a bed-wetting remedy, and books by Kyōzan, Hiroshige, Yoshitora, Tanekiyo etc. The bottom-left ad columns (新板端唄…, 川柳…地口…) and the second line of the bed-wetting ad are badly faded; prices for 白妙 and the bed-wetting cure are uncertain.
