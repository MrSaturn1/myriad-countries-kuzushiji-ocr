## notes
End of volume 3: Franklin explains he took Washington for a bandit, says he wishes to learn his name and asks him to tell his story; a note says the scene continues at the start of part 4, and a notice promises further tales of Washington, Adams and other heroes up to American independence; the left page is the publisher 甘泉堂's catalogue (Edo maps revised by 高井蘭山, almanacs, 庭訓往来, 百人一首 editions, warrior picture books). Unsure: first glyph of col 2 (〔な?〕かいせい, probably がいせい "peerless"), the faint ending ぶ〔まで?〕, the end of the map blurb, and the page number.
