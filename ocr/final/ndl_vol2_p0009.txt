## notes
Continues from the previous spread ("…the Dutch"): Dutch settlers went to the south of America and named their colony New Netherland (しんおらんだ); the English drove the Dutch out and renamed it New York (ねうよるく); Swedes (すへしあ) settled near New Netherland; the Dutch then took it all, the English expelled the Dutch again, and an Englishman named William (ういるれむ), sent as commander to that land, captured many pirates/bandits (〔か?〕いぞく). The caption says an Englishman petitioned the Queen to open a new country. The short columns at the foot of block C are uncertain even in the clearer Waseda copy (ねふゆるせい may be New Jersey); ▲△ is a stacked solid-over-hollow triangle mark; page numbers 七 (R) and 五? (L) are as printed/visible.
