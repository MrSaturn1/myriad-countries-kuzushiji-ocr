## notes
Title page of 万国噺 part 4, lower volume (Robun author, Yoshitora artist, Kinkyōdō publisher), with labelled Western objects: a sweet dish (コンペイトウ), a wine vessel and a candle-stand; on the left, Robun's preface (autumn of Bunkyū 1, 1861): the gods made the land; Columbus (閣龍) found the New World, Amerigo (墨利屈) followed, and North and South America took shape; George Washington (話聖東業阿尓熱) freed the colonies from British misrule and founded an independent nation. Unsure: the faded first title character(s) (probably をさな = 童), the furigana げおるげ, and the worn ends of the last two text columns (also smudged in the Waseda copy, whose left page matches; its right page is a different fan picture).
