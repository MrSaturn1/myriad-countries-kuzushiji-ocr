## notes
Right: title page of part 2, lower volume (Kanagaki Robun, Ichimōsai Yoshitora, publisher Yamadaya) with a chess-problem diagram; left: Robun's preface dated spring 1861 (辛酉), saying that where children once enjoyed the old red-book tale of Asahina's island voyages to the lands of the long-legged and long-armed, today's children want more than sweets, so he renders hearsay learning about the nations into kana. The preface sits on a picture of the balloon at Washington, North America (北亞墨利加合衆國 華盛都府 風船之圖). The ALT scan's right page differs (it pairs the preface with the Holland/Nanjing picture) but its left page matches and confirmed the last column. The balloon banner's Latin letters are garbled and upside down (■); unsure: the furigana for 小人, 春日, 脚色 and 品/替, and な at the end of col. 6.
