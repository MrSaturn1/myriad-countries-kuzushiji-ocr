## notes
Peter personally directs the building of warships mounting sixty cannon, devotes himself to every branch of learning, never neglects even a minor skill but tries it himself, and even masters medicine; caption: the Russian emperor, disguised as a woodcutter, works at Zaandam (さんだむ). Unsure: end of column 9 (printed like "のぐ", read as がく of がくもん); "こゝろを" has a faint ゝ.
