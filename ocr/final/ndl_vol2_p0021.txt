## notes
On American social welfare and schooling: people without support (widows, orphans) enter a relief house where each is given work suited to them, and everything they make is stored in the official storehouse; children born there are taught by teachers, so there are no beggars anywhere in the United States; every village has a school funded by its wealthy (or by the authorities if none), where boys and girls study from dawn till dusk, girls also learning sewing from women teachers, and rich and poor attend alike.
