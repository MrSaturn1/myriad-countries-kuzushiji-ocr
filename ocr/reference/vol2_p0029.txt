## notes
(a) Summary: The spread finishes the episode of young Washington (わしんとん) at Franklin's (ふらんきりん) house. Franklin's son reports what happened and the listeners shudder. Washington thanks Franklin and his son, but says his parents will be anxious, and promises to come back soon to thank them properly. They send him off in a carriage with villagers walking around it, and crowds line the road to see him. Near his village he gets down, sends the escort back, and hurries home alone to ふあきす (Fairfax?). "The story divides here": in the same country, at Norfolk (のるほるく), lives a deeply filial boy, あゝだむす (Adams). His father is dead, and he serves his mother so well that he equals the Twenty-Four Paragons of China. Now 18, he has a mother past sixty who is bent and weak and cannot walk, so she stays shut indoors. In the third month the hills are said to be in bloom. She laments that she cannot go to see the flowers and says she would rather die soon; her son is moved. "Fortunately (さいはひ) the weather is fine today," he says. He packs a lunch box (わりごさゝへ), carries her on his back to the north hill of ほるつもうと (Portsmouth?), seats her on a stump, and they eat and enjoy the blossoms. She is delighted, spots flowers blooming in the valley that smell finer than all the rest, and asks him to break off a branch (continued, つぎへ). Illustrations: Western figures on a cliff edge (L, top left), and a huge dragon or serpent among rocks and blossoming trees across both lower halves.
Layout: R top block, which opens with a boxed つゞき, then the L top-right block. This wraps around the palm-tree picture: from けふはそらも… the columns start lower and continue through ながめてしばしこれにをりかくて▲. Then the ▲ bottom-left block (▲はゝ / おやは … とりてよけふのはな) ends at the boxed つぎへ. はなし一ツにわかる (R, col. 21) is printed boxed, inline in the column. Kana reading order of the OCRs is right. No furigana other than the 車馬 gloss.
Key decisions:
- 車馬《〔くるま?〕》 (twice): under 車馬, two tiny glyphs are printed side by side as a warichu-style gloss. OCRs read them as 頭/興/職/むゝ. My best reading is くる | ま, but the glyphs are not legible.
- あゝだむす (5 times): after あ there is a short separate down-right stroke, which I read as ゝ, then a く-shaped cursive だ with dakuten. The stroke could instead be a flourish of あ, so あだむす (=Adams) is possible (about 30% chance).
- 今年 (clear 年; Lite reads 今とし).
- さいはひ (幸ひ; the second glyph is the two-stroke い; all OCRs read さははひ).
- きくもの身: "もの" is a single ligature glyph, with no ゝ.
- こしにつけたる: printed with the と-like hentaigana こ; Lite/v3 read とし.
- 柱: 万国四 is cut at the fold on both pages (left halves on R, right halves on L). The page number on L is clearly 八 (right stroke visible in both copies). R shows only the left half of a cross-stroke glyph, read as 〔七?〕 from the sequence.
The NDL copy (vol4/p0011) was confirmed visually to be the same spread and was the main source. The bottom-left block is worn and creased in the NDL copy, so Waseda was used there.
(b) Confidence: about 96% of characters are sure. The weak points are the two 車馬 glosses, the ゝ in the five あゝだむす, the R page number, ▲はゝ (a single worn glyph group), the middle glyph of つぎへ, and the dakuten choices in ひと〴〵 and ゆる〳〵.
(c) Candidates. Counts are against roughly 1300 normalized reference characters, with 。▲〽, furigana and □ ignored. "Edit distance" is the Levenshtein distance over the whole page. "Bag diff" ignores order and counts characters that are wrong, missing or extra.

The R col. 14 line "のらうにやく男女…かいま見んと" is spliced in after さいくわいをやくしつゝ, and it skips あゝだむす今とし十八…ならざればたゞいへにのみとぢこもり身をなぐさむ (about 50 characters). It drops 〳〵/〴〵 throughout and the dakuten in some places. Misreads: 車馬頭, おくで, ゆるおんれは, さははひ, かて月四日 for かくて ▲はゝおやは, いへのこ in the wrong place, 5 □.
- NDL v3 on NDL scan: similar. It has the right block order, but it drops 〳〵, misreads (車馬興, つとぎやう, ここび, あらたむす, よろい), and it garbles the worn ▲ block (□□はく, なづむつ, at least 12 □). The text after けふのはな is lost.
- NDL Lite on Waseda: good characters with the 〳〵 marks and 〽 kept, but the line order is jumbled (the L top block's last lines and the middle block are interleaved, and one line is a 39-□ run). Misreads: 車馬むゝ, わしるとん, しゆづらい, さははひ, あらだむす, まつしも…なりのころ, はゝうへ ordering.
- NDL Lite on NDL scan: the best. The line order is nearly perfect and the characters are mostly right. Errors: 太良四四 header noise, おてれい, 車馬職, 今とし (for 今年), さははひ, らたてさ, あらりと, ながむんに, こゞめ, つぎん, and a few fragmentary lines in the worn ▲ block (はら / をなぐさめよろで).
- Claude by eye: only fragments are right. It gets 車馬, 男女, 今年十八 (correctly 年) and the 万国四 柱. Most kana are wrong, and the L page is largely ■.
