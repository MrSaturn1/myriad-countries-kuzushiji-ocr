## notes
Image check: the NDL scan (p0019) is the same spread as Waseda p0020 (same block, same cuts); all readings were made primarily from the NDL scan, cross-checked against Waseda. The column hidden in the Waseda gutter is the right page's last column 「ものなりといふみかどゝ」, fully legible on NDL.
Reading order across the spread: R top block (つゞき … みかどゝ) → L top block (まじはるもの … はげむ▲) → R bottom block (▲みかど … おほ) → L bottom block (よそ … いりようの［つゞく］). The L bottom block is set in short stepped columns around the figures; each line above is one printed column.
Hentaigana notes: こ is frequently written with the 古-derived form that looks like と (すまふこの, このものを, ぞくこく, かへるこの, おこせし, こゝに); these are normalized to こ. こと〴〵く (R col 4, L cols 17–18) is printed as こと + a single ぐ-shaped iteration mark + く; transcribed with the standard 〴〵. しらんど has a visible dakuten on と in the print (kept). 一チげい is printed with katakana チ (kept).
Uncertain points: R col 2 う〔つ?〕む — the second glyph is a ろ/つ-shaped stroke; sense would favour うかむ (浮かむ, 可-form か), all OCR read つ. R col 8 わ〔う?〕 — read as わう (王, "the king's ships"); OCRs give わる/わろ/わか; う and ろ are nearly identical in this hand. R col 11 あるす〔ひ?〕だ — foreign name (あるすひだ・みつど?), glyph count is tight. L bottom 〔う?〕へじばん — top glyph is a small ∠-shaped stroke; meaning of じばん unclear. The continuation box at the end is つ + a ゞ-like zigzag + く-like stroke: read つゞく (NDL Lite reads つぎへ). 丁付 numbers on both fore-edges are mostly lost in the fold: right shows strokes consistent with 十五, left with 十六. 柱 reads 万国初 on both (国 is a simplified form; could be 國). ○四 is a circled 四 with small circle above, top centre of the left page frame.

(a) Summary: The spread continues the life of Peter the Great: wishing to learn seamanship, he disguised himself as a ship's hand, went to England and London and lived at the (royal?) shipyard workshop, where the 600-odd skilled craftsmen and courtiers all praised his ability; he then left England, passed through Holland, Dresden and Vienna, and was about to go to Italy when rebels rose to depose him, so he returned home, the revolt having already been put down by the Russian Gordon. Still angry, he punished all the rebels; a certain Menshikov (めれしこう), talented and loyal, was promoted by the emperor, who declared that governing the country and giving peace to the people was the first duty, reduced the domestic annual tax, and for the country's profit began [projects] in various countries. The illustration caption reads "Russian peasants working at the way of farming."
(b) Confidence: about 95% of characters (roughly 510 of 535 base-text kana/kanji, excluding furigana and markup) I am sure of; the remaining ~5% are the 〔?〕 items above plus a few individual kana accepted from multi-candidate agreement where the print is cramped (e.g. あるすひだ, から, がために, the 百姓/耕作 furigana).
(c) Candidate errors (approximate counts against this reference, main text + caption):
- v3 on Waseda (ndl_v3/waseda): good order, but misses the whole gutter column (ものなりといふみかどゝ) and half of まじはるもの (□□まし□□), drops the R-bottom/L-bottom short columns partly (ひみづからいへるやうおほよそくにをおさめ lost or scrambled), garbles the caption into the text (魯西の百姓耕作施す), prefixes margin noise 才目衣; misreads ありきま, わる, すまふと, すびた, いきりす, をさる, すへて, らたりや, いたらんことほつしする, みるど, すてに, たびらぐ, そのちん, めれみちこう, つらし(へらし), くにのそろは(えきを), ちりよう, つきへ; drops 〴〵 and べき/まし. Roughly 60–75 wrong/missing characters.
