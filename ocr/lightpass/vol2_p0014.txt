## notes
Merriken and Harina are blamed when the treasured jewel is smashed; Merriken is about to kill himself when Harina stops him, and the official Florey reports to the King. Volume 3 opens by retelling this: the Queen's council spares them from execution because Merriken descends from Americus and Harina from Columbus, so they are exiled to Carolina in North America with 300 convicts, sailing from the Thames, until a storm hits near "Harumata" (Bermuda?). Unsure spots: 萬死〔ばん?〕に (one glyph between 死 and に); 〔とふちんこ?〕づから (maybe とてもみづから); 国王〔野に?〕 at the end of the right page; the right-margin running title 〔万画?〕三 and a cut-off page number 十■; ころんし (ころん氏?).
