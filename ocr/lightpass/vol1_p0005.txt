## notes
Right: Holland — its people excel in crafts, trade, astronomy, geography and natural science, and gather rare goods from every land to profit by commerce (figures: a Dutchman and a Nanjing Chinese). Left: Robun's preface (spring 1861) over a picture of a Washington balloon — old picture books about Asahina's island tours no longer satisfy today's children, so he retells "world tales" in kana. Unsure: left-page 柱 number (〔二?〕), furigana of 宜 (よき) and 脚色 (しくみ), first kana of わしんとんふ; Roman lettering on the balloon band left untranscribed.
