## notes
Peter the Great (unnamed "みかど" of Russia) learns shipbuilding disguised as a boatman in London, with 600 skilled followers; he tours Holland, Dresden, Vienna, and heads for Italy but returns when a revolt breaks out; Gordon (ごるとん) has already crushed it, but the emperor punishes the rebels; Menshikov (めれしこう) serves loyally; the emperor rules diligently, lowers taxes and promotes national profit. Unsure: うかむ (OCR read うつむ), たしみ, その (hentaigana) ふねの, あるあひだ (OCR すひだ), ものあり (OCR あか), and the last columns of L bottom (〔あ?〕しよこくの / 〔三?〕人じばんをはし / いりよう) are worn and only tentatively read; page numbers half-hidden in the gutter.
