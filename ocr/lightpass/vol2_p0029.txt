## notes
Washington takes leave of Franklin's household, is escorted home in a carriage amid crowds, and returns alone to his home in "ふあきす" (Fairfax); a new story then begins about the filial youth Adams (あゝだむす) of "のるほるく", who carries his aged, lame mother on his back to view the blossoms on the northern hill of "ほるつもうと". Unsure: the small glyph after 車馬 (twice; read as ruby ばしや), さ〔い〕はひ in L col 12, and the worn ends of L-bottom cols 10–11 (たに〔そこ〕, その〔かをり〕) read from the Waseda scan; 国/國 in the 柱 is unclear; かしこ in L line 7 and にははな in L line 8 kept from Lite without full confirmation.
