**Left page: preface.**

The two first spirits came down from heaven, stirred the waters of the sea, and made the lands. In the five continents some peoples settled early and some late, but all within the four seas are brothers, and the sun and moon are father and mother to all. How could that truth be any different there? Columbus discovered the New World, Amerigo followed him, and the whole continent was mapped out. That is North and South America. Then Westerners came to this land. Its people multiplied, its trades and industries grew, and nothing was lacking. In the United States of the northern continent there was a man called George Washington, who had all three virtues. In loyalty he took no thought for his own life; in government he never let the nation lose its dignity. In the end he freed his country from England's harsh rule and passed the glory of an independent nation on to later ages. […] I have strung his deeds together as a popular tale and made it a picture-book for children […].

Autumn of Bunkyū 1 (1861), the Year of the Rooster. Written by Kanagaki Robun.
